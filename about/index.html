A little about me.

I’m Nemath, a co-founder of Shifu Health in San Francisco. Most of my time goes into building the company. I write when I can and make films, photographs, and music on the side.

the work

At Shifu Health, we’re trying to make good health support more accessible. It is still early. A lot of the job is talking to people, getting things wrong, correcting course, and building the next version.

Before Shifu, I worked across AI research, data systems, and a few earlier attempts at building companies. I completed my graduate studies at Georgia Tech while trying to balance research, internships, a startup, boxing, and everything else. I did not balance it especially well, but I learned a lot.

the practice

I’ve written on and off for years. I tend to come back to it when I need to slow down and understand what I actually think. I’m trying to make it a regular practice again.

The older pieces stay here too. I would write some of them differently now, but removing them would make the archive less honest.

When I have time, I also make short films at @bynemath, take photographs, play music, and train. This site is simply where I keep those things together.
