03

working notes

Small lessons from building

field note 01

Go where the work happens.

Visiting medical facilities made the back office legible in a way another round of virtual outreach never could. Proximity changes the quality of the questions.

field note 02

Build alongside people.

Collecting requirements once and disappearing to build is not customer discovery. Keep the feedback loop open while the product is still easy to change.

field note 03

Work backwards from tonight.

When a problem expands, return to the deadline: if this needs to work tonight, what must happen right now? Urgency can be clarifying when the outcome is concrete.
